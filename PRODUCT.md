# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, React islands, shadcn/ui, pnpm and Playwright, as recorded in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Users

People preparing application documents, product photos and everyday social media images in Indonesia and internationally.

## Product Purpose

Help people prepare images and documents locally in their browser, with accurate output and understandable limitations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

File processing stays on the device. No account, backend processing, paid API or file persistence. The tool catalog and its per-tool limits are defined in `src/tool-registry.ts`.

## Brand Commitments

Doculven; clear Indonesian and English; the Nordic Emerald & Slate palette and editorial studio direction in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA and usable keyboard and mobile workflows.
